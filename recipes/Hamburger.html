Seth's Hamburger Recipe

Home

Description

Seth's Hamburger is a delicious homemade burger made with a juicy, well-seasoned beef patty, fresh vegetables, melted cheese and soft burger buns. Carefully prepared with simple ingredients and a blend of tasty seasonings, it's the perfect combination of flavour, freshness and satisfaction in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 2 hamburger buns
  • 250g ground beef
  • 2 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 1 medium tomato
  • ½ onion
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon mustard (optional)
  • 2–4 pickle slices (optional)
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil or butter

Steps

  1. Prepare the vegetables: Wash the lettuce and tomato. Slice the tomato and onion into thin rings.
  2. Prepare the beef: Place the ground beef in a bowl. Add salt and black pepper, then mix gently and shape into two round patties.
  3. Cook the patties: Heat a frying pan over medium heat and add a little cooking oil. Cook the beef patties until thoroughly cooked, turning them as needed.
  4. Add the cheese: Place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty near the end of cooking and allow it to melt.
  5. Toast the buns: Cut the hamburger buns into two halves and lightly toast the inner sides with a little butter.
  6. Prepare the sauce: Mix mayonnaise, ketchup and mustard in a small bowl.
  7. Assemble the burger: Spread the sauce on the bottom half of each bun. Add lettuce, tomato slices, onion rings and the cooked beef patty.
  8. Complete the burger: Add pickle slices if desired, spread a little more sauce and cover with the top half of the bun.
  9. Serve: Place the hamburgers on a serving plate and enjoy while warm
