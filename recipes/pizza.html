Description

Experience the timeless taste of perfection with Seth's Classic Cheese Pizza! Made with a soft, golden crust, rich tomato sauce, and a generous layer of melted, stretchy mozzarella cheese, every bite delivers a delicious blend of creamy, savoury flavours.

Baked to golden perfection, it's a simple yet irresistible classic made for every pizza lover

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 3⁄4cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1⁄2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil or olive oil
  • 1⁄2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1-2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1⁄2teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1⁄2 teapsoon garlic powder (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough: In a bowl, combine warm water, yeast and sugar. Allow it to sit for about 5 minutes.
  2. Mix the ingredients: Add flour, salt and vegetable oil. Mix until a soft dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough:Knead on a clean surface for about 5–8 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Let it rise: Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover it and leave for about 1 hour or until it doubles in size.
  5. Prepare the oven: your oven to 220°C (425°F).
  6. Shape the pizza: Roll out the dough into a circular shape and place it on a lightly greased baking tray
  7. Add the sauce:Spread tomato sauce evenly over the pizza base, leaving a little space around the edges.
  8. Add the cheese: Sprinkle a generous amount of shredded mozzarella cheese over the tomato sauce.
  9. Season: Add oregano and garlic powder for extra flavour.
  10. Bake: Place the pizza in the preheated oven and bake for 12–15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  11. Serve:Remove from the oven, allow it to cool slightly, slice into pieces and enjoy your homemade Seth's Classic Cheese Pizza!
